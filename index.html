A new way through a podcast.

The map

Every episode is a star. The lines are doors.

Six territories, from the shadow to the numinous, each in its own colour. Tap a star and its neighbours light up: related episodes, each with a spoken reason why they belong together.

Journeys

Follow a path, one stop at a time.

Five curated journeys put the episodes in an order that builds. When one ends, the next stop plays. Put a journey down and pick it up weeks later; your place is kept.

  1. The Map of the Psyche 15 stops
  2. Shadow, Self & Individuation 11 stops
  3. Dreams, Symbols & the Numinous 13 stops
  4. Jung at the Movies 8 stops
  5. Jung in Everyday Life 14 stops

Search

Search the words actually spoken.

Every episode has a transcript, and the app searches all of them. Type nightmares and you get the sentence, the episode, and the moment. One tap and you are listening from there.

Your guide

Hi, I'm Marcus.

A few years ago I fell into Jung's Collected Works and never quite climbed back out. So I started a Meetup group, and then this podcast, to think through his ideas out loud with other people. Every episode grew out of a live event: real questions, honest discussion, and Jung's own words at the center.

Pick what pulls you.
